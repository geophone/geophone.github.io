I've found the best way to improve at anything is to set your sights then partake in that activity.

Fundamentally experience leads to skill but It's also been essential in my experience to study the best.  If you're not observing someone more skilled in the discipline in which you're practicing/improving it will be difficult to crack that next bar.

These days often AI is used as an oracle of 'the most skilled' or peak at a given activity and used as a learning and technique oracle.  I would expect to see more of this in software engineering at a high level in the future.

Lastly it's important to review your own experience of whatever activity it is you're seeking to improve.

I find this trifecta in my own experience to be extremely useful in improving at a strategy or skill based activity.
